# Charlie / PIXIE social-network DJ character

## Canonical distinction

Charlie is a fictional creative character used to explore the social-network DJ workflow inside PIXIE Creator OS.

The separate Charlie J material in `50-ways-to-leave-another` is a real-person case study and remains explicitly unapproved, non-operational, and non-endorsed. This PIXIE character must not be presented as Charlie J, a Charlie J product, or evidence of a relationship with Charlie J.

## Character pathway

The `50 Ways to Leave Another` project provides a music-first laboratory for characters, songs, recording decisions, rights questions, and creative development. Within that fictional layer, Charlie is developing a practice around creating, organizing, performing, and publishing music on the social web.

PIXIE Creator OS is her fictional workstation.

## Why the music matters

The project gives the operating system a creative loop to model:

1. Create or develop music.
2. Record and organize source material.
3. Build a crate and prepare a set.
4. Rehearse with an external DJ engine such as Mixxx and compatible hardware.
5. Capture live/performance state without pretending a broadcast occurred.
6. Prepare a social record through the AT Protocol adapter.
7. Prepare audio publishing through the plyr.fm adapter.
8. Keep rights, contributors, masters, publishing, and release state explicit before anything becomes a real release.

## Cross-project rule

The 50 Ways project can reference PIXIE as a **fictional workflow extension** of the Charlie character. PIXIE can reference the 50 Ways material as a **character/design case study**. Neither repository should imply that the real Charlie J has reviewed, approved, endorsed, commissioned, or adopted PIXIE Creator OS.
