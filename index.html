Available apps

Connections, permissions and installation

Available means a catalog destination is offered; live service health is not monitored. Added means a launch shortcut. Connected means PIXIE has an active, explicitly approved integration; it never means permission to control every Atmosphere app. Authorization required means a supported operation still needs your consent. Unavailable means a route cannot currently be offered. Unsupported means PIXIE has no implementation for that operation.

Removing a shortcut does not revoke permissions at its provider. Disconnect the Companion project below to clear its context; use each other app or your account host to review and revoke its own grants. No third-party OAuth write adapter is implemented here.

No app installer is implemented. For native apps or Add to Home Screen, follow the target app’s own platform guidance. A PIXIE shortcut is not an installation.
